The Frontier Overhaul mod currently includes:

    * Nachos' One-Handed Wallrun
    * Nachos' Weapon Script Fixes
    * Nachos' Rebreather Masks for Militia Grunts
    * ASillyNeko's Extended Attrition

* These come included because, taken separately, they will cause scripting conflicts with FO and break the game.
* This also serves as an unofficial modpack for R1Delta.
