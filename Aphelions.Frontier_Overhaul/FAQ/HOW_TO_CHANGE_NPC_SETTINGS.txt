//////////////////////////
/// MAX NUMBER OF NPCS ///
//////////////////////////

* Go into \scripts\vscripts\mp\_ai_game_modes
* ctrl + f to find:
 	level.max_npc_per_side       <- 24
	level.max_npc_per_side_small <- 20

* To me, 24 feels about right for most maps, but you can raise or lower it to whatever you feel like.
* 20 is for smaller maps that feel overcrowded with lots of minions.
* 12 and 9 are the defaults for when there are too many bots/players

///////////////////////
//// ADJUST NPC AI ////
///////////////////////

* Go into \scripts\vscripts\mp\_ai_soldiers
* ctrl + f to find:
    function UpdateAILethality( soldier, enemy )

* The enemy accuracy and proficiency will change depending on the difficulty.
* As far as I can tell, 4 seems to be the highest meaningful value.
* This does not affect the bot Pilots.

//////////////////////////
/// NPC TITANS PER TEAM ///
//////////////////////////

* Go into \scripts\vscripts\mp\_ai_npc_titans
* ctrl + f to find:
 	function ShouldSpawnPilotWithTitan( team ) // Titan Spawns per Team

* The limits will be inside the function.
* Your team will have a different number of NPC Titans in Attrition & Hardpoint depending on the AI Lethality.
* Including you, Normal is a 4v4, High is a 4v5, and Very High is a 4v6

* You can also go into \scripts\vscripts\mp\_ai_npc_titans
* ctrl + f to find:
	wait RandomFloat( 20, 90 )  // Titan spawn delay in seconds

* This makes it so that Titans don't immediately spawn when the game starts or when they are killed.
* This does NOT affect the bot Titans.

/////////////////////////
/// SPECIAL NPC WAVES ///
/////////////////////////

* Sniper Spectres, Suicide Spectres, Cloak Drones, Ghost Pilots, Gunships have a chance to spawn in waves.

* Go into \scripts\vscripts\mp\_ai_game_modes
* ctrl + f to find:
    function SuicideSpectreWaveThink( team )
    function GunshipWaveThink( team )
    And so on...

* From here, you can adjust how often waves happen and how many NPCs they spawn

////////////////////////
/// CONSOLE COMMANDS ///
////////////////////////

* Press ~ to open the console ( 1 means enabled, 0 means disabled )

    delta_play_hitsounds 0 (disables the distracting sfx when shooting enemies)
    delta_play_killsounds 0  (same but for killing enemies)
    sv_cheats 1; notarget; noclip; (makes NPCs not target you and enables free roam)
