//////////////////////////////
////// PILOT PRIMARIES ///////
//////////////////////////////

SUPPRESSORS:
* Suppressors have been tweaked across the board to be more viable, especially with secondaries and snipers
* Getting a kill with a suppressor on an enemy who doesn't have line of sight on you will reward you a +20xp "Stealth Bonus"

ARC GRENADES:
* Will only insta-kill grunts, takes two to kill NPC Pilots and Grunt Captains

R101-C CARBINE:
* Small increase to ADS recoil
* Small increase to weapon spread

EVA-8 SHOTGUN:
* Run-n-Gun kit now applies
* Slightly buffed the range for the player
* Headshots have been enabled
* Leadwall mod reduces damage by only 9% instead of 17% (i.e. the Twin-B version)
* Added a Full-Auto mod (fire rate is a bit slower than the Burn Card version)
* Added a Pump-Action mod that does more damage with higher range but with a slower fire rate

R1-S BURRELL SMG:
* Restored cut SMG version of the R101. Still experimenting with its role and numbers
* Temporarily removed because it will actually break the game super hard if you try and use it outside the mod

HEMLOK:
* Reduced weapon spread

VALKYRIE:
* Slight damage buff to headshots (x2 instead of x1.4)
* Added the dark mode skin, since its base model is not very detailed or interesting. Black makes everything better >:)

SPITFIRE:
* Reduced ADS time from 0.7 sec to 0.5 sec
* Range buff
* Added Cyclic Receiver mod (ADS and reload are slower than the Burn Card version)

KRABER:
* Added Anti-Titan Rounds mod (has less ammo than the burn card version)

///// PILOT SECONDARIES //////
//////////////////////////////
P2011:
* Will 3-shot Pilots and Grunts at close range instead of 4
* Burn card version adds an 3 extra rounds to your mag

RE-45:
* Will 5-shot Pilots at close range instead of 6
* Starburst now has slightly reduced recoil, better fire rate, and +5 extra mag size

TWIN-B:
* Does enough damage to one-shot grunt captains (250 health) at close range 
* Added Double Tap mod to fire both barrels (almost) at once
