This is my repository for R1Delta mods.
To install, download this repo in bulk, extract the zip file, and simply place the desired mod folders into your addons folder in R1D.

A few notes regarding Frontier Overhaul and other mods:
    * If you install the Frontier Overhaul mod, know that it comes built-in with FO-friendly versions of both the Instant Evac mod and the Item Descriptions mod.
    * This is because FO has several exclusive weapon mods that the vanilla Item Descriptions mod cannot account for, hence why it was built in.
    * The evac mod shown here is also -exclusively- meant for the owners of multiplayer servers.
    * There are several mods made by other people that FO comes built-in with in order to avoid scripting conflicts, so when in doubt, always check the README.

Other than that, Custom Backgrounds and Extra Music will work just fine alongside FO.

Good luck, have fun!
